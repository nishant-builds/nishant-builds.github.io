## Facebook example
- 1 of the important problems: People signing up but not coming back - “Low Activation and Retention”
- Leaking bucket problem - new users are coming but retention is bad
- Daily Active Users = New users today + Old Users
- How to solve it? Understand the problem 1st - not randomly propose solutions
  - Talk to users - Empathise with the users - Meet users
  - Observe users
  - Market research
  - Analyze competitors
  - Talk to team, stakeholders
- FB’s insight - People who made > 7 friends in 1st 10 days : Had higher retention rate (More reasons to come back to the platform, talk to friends, look at their pages etc.)
- What FB did after this ?
  - FB came with the feature: “People you may know”. 2008-09 - No API to sync contacts with FB then
  - In onboarding step - they started asking which school, what is passing out year : Used this data to recommend and connect
  - Another feature: “Home feed” - collected everything friends are doing and put it on home page(no need now to go to individual’s home page) - Makes it easier for them to interact

## The one and only rule of Product Management
- Not science, everything is subjective, opinions matter - No single truth
- As a PM, you exist to solve customer problems and achieve business outcomes
- Customer problem - Want to connect with my friends and interact them easily
- Business problem - Increase customer retention and increase revenue
- Business → “Value” to customers. Customers → either “money or attention” to business

## What is value? 
- “Problem or desire” in the life of the user - which you can solve
- Every product can be broken in two parts - Problem space + Solution space. Problem space is only about users. They don’t care about the business

## Sources of ideas:
- Inputs from business team
- Market research on
  - Trends
  - Competitors
- Talk to stakeholders. 2 type of stakeholders
  - Internal stakeholders - Developers and designers
  - External stakeholders - Sales, Marketing, Partners
- Data 
  - Observe users of the existing product (see stats, metrics)
  - Talk to users
- Own ideas

## Need to prioritize & Product roadmap
- Can’t execute all ideas - Bandwidth constraint. Need to prioritize
- Before investing your time and effort - Break every idea into - Problem + Solution and Think about 3 things:
  - What’s the user value ? Problem & Solution good enough for the user?
  - Business Value - Why should the business do it ? Justifies the ROI ?
  - Feasibility - Does my team have the capability ? - This is where AI helps most
- Post analyzing above -
  - Most of the ideas will go into trash can - Say No
  - All the Yes - We will have to prioritize (Some will be implemented, some will be kept for later)
  - Post prioritization → Selected ideas → Product Roadmap (High level plan for the product)

***Product management is a team sport. Until some time back - The team consisted of “people” only. Now it also has - “AI agents”***

## Process of converting the product roadmap into actual product & PRD, Backlog, Sprint, Standup:
- For every initiative of the roadmap we will create a PRD (Product requirement document). Writing helps in alignment and clarity
- PRD components:
  - Why ? User & Business objective
  - What ? Flow of the product
  - How ? Success metrics
  - Other details
- Developers don’t want to read PRD every day. They care about the tasks they have to work upon a daily level. You make - Backlog (essentially a to-do list)
- SPRINT: To not overburden, not making very long goals and dumping all tasks at once → We take a time of 2-3 weeks. You decide what tasks will be picked up from the backlog (sprint backlog). After 2 weeks - meeting to review (sprint review meeting)
- STANDUP → Daily stand-up before starting work to hold people accountable, review, and remove blockers. 3 questions: What have you done yesterday ? What are you going to do today ? Any blockers ?

## Once the product is made → Launch the product
- Create a GTM (Go to Market) strategy. Stakeholders - Growth teams (Marketing - Creates demand, Sales - Sells), Customer support team
- User users product
- User generates data → Gives feedback
- Cycle reiterates. Keeps going in loop

***This is the product management life-cycle***

## 3 parts of product management life-cycle:
- Product Discovery: Steps till PRD. You decide what to build
- Product Delivery: PRD se making. Work with partners to make the product
- Product Distribution: When you take the product to users and get their feedback
