## NoBroker Story
- Consultants - Years- 6m-1yr
- Stayed in rented apartments
- Problem: Need broker → Broker takes brokerage charges
- Their idea: Use internet → Let owners list their properties, users can directly contact without any brokerage
- Launched -> But not as successful
- They started talking to users. Insight:
  - Brokers do not only do property discovery
  - But also with police verification, documentation
  - People who had listed the properties lived outside, were busy - No time to show property
  - They used broker as a caretaker of the property, negotiator
- They understood. They pivoted. Instead of just a website - they changed them into a 'managed service' company for both owners as well as users
- Rather than thinking - I am going to only create a platform to connect
- They focussed on what customers want from them
- They owned the customer journey:
  - Builders (Built many flats -> need to sell)
  - Owners (Need to give rent)
  - Buyers (Want to buy flats)
  - Tenants (Need to take rent)
  - Service management <-> Rent Payments <-> Moving & Packing <-> Cleaning/Repairing services
- Now the market leader. All other real-estate tech contemporary companies have perished

## What is product sense ?
- Product sense is the ability to anticipate what users will value and make sound & practical decisions about what to build
- Product sense (by bringing clarity in the problem → leads to thoughtful solution)

### Problem space (Truly understand the problem)
- Problem - What's the problem ? Why does this matter ?
- Users - Who is affected and how ?
- Alternatives - How do they solve it now ? What's broken ?
- Business - Why is this worth solving ?
- Validation - What evidence confirms it's real ?

### Solution space (Unless truly understood, no right to solve it)
- Brainstorming - What solutions can solve the core issue ? 
- Solution fit ? aligns with users and business ?
- Risks - What are the trade-offs and constraints ?
- Metrics - How will we measure success ?
- Feasibility - Can we build and ship this realistically ?

- Listen to users but don't do exactly what they say. They also don't know what they really want. Henry Ford - If I would have asked users what they want - they would have asked for faster horses
- Users are not God. Consider them as your children
- You want the best for them - but you also know that they don't know enough (sometimes they may know but mostly not)

## Product Thinking = Problem Solving (How would you design this product from scratch ? How would you improve this product ? etc. etc.)
- Understand context (Business goal, Market situation, Constraints, Success Metric)
- Define the stakeholders/user segments & problems (Who is struggling ? What pain exists ? How important is it ?)
  - Not just problems
  - What are the desires (What do users want but are not able to articulate) ? What are the opportunities ?
- Explore solutions & trade-offs (What can we build, what should we avoid, what are the costs ?
  - To get ideas, be creative - you need to expose yourself to many products. Understand what's happening ?
  - Once you have enough ideas - Prioritization (effort x impact)
- Make a clear recommendation
  - Chosen solution, reasoning, risks and success metrics - WHat's the rationale ? What are the risks ? And how will you measure success ?
